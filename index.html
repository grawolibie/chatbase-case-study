How the rubric becomes an account strategy

The supplied research contains numerical criterion ratings for FOOTDISTRICT and narrative criterion assessments for the other nine accounts. Lead, secondary and discovery labels are strategic interpretations of those findings, not new calculated scores. The evidence-coverage percentages are not fit scores. The revised model uses 40% economic, 30% commercial and 30% AE opportunity, with A1–A4 equally weighted within Pillar 3; the historical A5 accessibility criterion is retained only as a practical check. Unknown support demand and buying intent remain qualification questions. Multiple economic angles can overlap: do not add their potential benefits without testing for double counting. Named stakeholders are research leads, not newly verified current contacts. Technology labels use the supplied detection snapshot; they do not establish which features are deployed.
